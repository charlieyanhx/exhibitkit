# Options risk
Options involve risk and are not suitable for all investors. Prior to buying or selling an option, a person must receive a copy of *Characteristics and Risks of Standardized Options*, available from the Options Clearing Corporation (theocc.com) or from any broker. Multi-leg option positions incur multiple transaction costs, carry assignment and early-exercise risk, may require substantial margin, and can lose more than any premium collected. Uncovered short options carry the risk of unlimited (calls) or very large (puts) loss. Delta hedging requires continuous monitoring and may not be executable at the assumed prices in fast markets. Strategies that sell volatility or skew are exposed to sudden, large losses in market stress, and their profits are typically concentrated in a small number of episodes.
